  2. 02
    Junior Data Scientist • Aug 2025 – Jan 2026 Public Sector & OCR

    Transforming macroeconomic publications and scanned budgets into structured, analytics-ready tidy data.

    Crucial macroeconomic reports and public sector budgets are often published as locked scanned PDFs and complex tables with merged headers. At the Ministry of Economy and Finance (MEF), I engineered an automated, modern Document Extraction system leveraging Datalab/Marker to extract tabular structures and text from scanned PDF reports. To solve structural anomalies, I designed a pipeline powered by LLM services to clean, normalize, and validate messy extracted outputs into structured datasets based on Hadley Wickham’s Tidy Data principles.

    • Engineered automated modern OCR table extraction pipeline converting complex PDF reports to structured data.
    • Implemented LLM validation pipelines to resolve merged headers, footnotes, and multi-currency formats.
    • Standardized macroeconomic data workflows according to Tidy Data principles for downstream economic modeling.
    Python Datalab / Marker LLM Normalization Pandas Tidy Data Streamlit
    Document Extraction Project
